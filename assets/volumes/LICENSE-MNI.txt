The 3D brain phantom (assets/volumes/brain3d.*) is derived from the ICBM 152 Nonlinear
Asymmetrical template version 2009c (MNI152NLin2009cAsym), obtained via TemplateFlow:
Fonov VS, Evans AC, McKinstry RC, Almli CR, Collins DL. Unbiased nonlinear average age-appropriate
brain templates from birth to adulthood. NeuroImage 2009;47:S102. Fonov V, Evans AC, Botteron K,
Almli CR, McKinstry RC, Collins DL. Unbiased average age-appropriate atlases for pediatric studies.
NeuroImage 2011;54(1):313-327. Ciric R et al. TemplateFlow. Nat Methods 2022;19:1568-1571.
